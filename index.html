Three tools I built

I design each one, build it with Claude Code, and run the hosting, databases and deploys myself.

  1. Role Radar

    My job search, run as software. It reads 14 job boards every day, keeps the openings in one dashboard, emails me a digest each morning and drafts a CV for the role I pick.

    14 job boards, read every day

    Next.js, TypeScript, Neon Postgres, GitHub Actions, Resend, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Anthropic SDK

  2. WorkHub

    A project management tool for a small team: tasks and deadlines, a leads pipeline, AI-written meeting minutes and end-of-day wrap-ups. It also tracks what the AI costs, by day and by kind of work.

    6 projects, 50+ tasks and 160+ leads in it today

    AI meeting minutes, daily wrap-ups, leads pipeline, AI spend tracking

  3. AI receptionist

    A voice receptionist for clinics. It answers the phone in Hindi, English or a mix of both, and books appointment slots. On live test calls it cost about Rs. 3 a call.

    About Rs. 2,500 a month for a clinic taking 600 calls

    Voice calls, slot booking, cost tracked per call

A tenth of the cost

WorkHub writes a research report on every new lead, and each report costs money in AI fees. In its first two days a report cost $3.31. From 18 November I had it at about $0.33, a 90% cut, across 76 reports.
